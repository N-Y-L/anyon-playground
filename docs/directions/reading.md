# Reading notes and talks

The references below extend three calculations from these notes: saddle scattering, wire end modes and statistical inference from a detector signal. In each case, a specific calculation connects the shorter treatment here to the longer paper.

## Three calculations and their sources

**Dilation and scattering.** The [single-particle saddle](saddle.html) connects directly to [Subramanyan, Hegde, Vishveshwara and Bradlyn's inverted-oscillator article](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.09875). Its dilation and scattering sections develop the same equations in several physical settings. The preparation, observable and boundary conditions identify which parts of the comparison carry over between systems.

**Majorana pairing on a short chain.** [Kitaev's quantum-wire paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0010440) gives the starting point for the [wire calculation](wires.html). At $\mu=0$ and $\Delta=w>0$, the Hamiltonian pairs Majorana operators between neighboring sites, leaving one unpaired operator at each end. A four-site chain is already enough to display that pairing. [Hegde and Vishveshwara](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.03394) develop the connection to oscillations, decay and potential landscapes away from this limit.

**Exchange in a detector signal.** [Vishveshwara's correlation proposal](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0304568) and [Nakamura and collaborators' interferometry experiment](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-020-1019-1) approach statistics through different motions and observables. A device diagram with the measured quantity attached to its contacts makes the distinction concrete. The [detector notes](detectors.html) collect the corresponding amplitude and phase comparisons.

## Longer companions

[David Tong's quantum Hall lectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06687) give a continuous account from Landau levels to the fractional effect and field-theory descriptions. The wavefunction construction, quasiholes and effective descriptions provide context for the calculations used here.

[Feldman and Halperin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.08998) offer a detailed account of fractional charge, statistics and the logic of their detection. It is particularly useful when two papers use similar words for different measurement protocols. Its experimental survey covers work through its publication date; device-specific claims are documented in the original studies.

[Kitaev's honeycomb-model article](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0506438) connects a physical lattice model to the structure of fusion and braiding. Its mathematical appendices supply the definitions behind the $F$ and $R$ operations used in the exchange calculations.

## Talks and associated questions

[Smitha Vishveshwara's Rutgers seminar, “From nano-constrictions to event horizons”](https://cmsr.rutgers.edu/newsevents-cmsr/event-details/1161-smitha-vishveshwara-from-nano-constrictions-to-event-horizons-facets-of-the-inverted-harmonic-oscillator), has a recording linked on the event page. The connection is through the inverted oscillator: exponential stretching, scattering probabilities with a thermal form, and gravitational ringdown involve distinct physical assumptions. The saddle figure isolates the stretching and its effect on a fixed-mode overlap.

[The Perimeter talk “Braiding statistics of loops in three spatial dimensions”](https://pirsa.org/14120034) accompanies the [loop-braiding calculation](statistics.html#loops-have-motions-that-points-do-not). The linked-loop process raises a geometric question: which deformation ceases to be allowed when the third loop remains present?

Smitha Vishveshwara's [Illinois research page](https://physics.illinois.edu/people/directory/profile/smivish) places these connections in the broader setting of topological dynamics, ultracold gases and gravitation.

## Questions left by these calculations

- **Saddle:** how does the late-time signal change when finite detector acceptance replaces the coherent-mode projector, with the same prepared packet?
- **Wire ramp:** how do ramp duration and wire length affect the energy above the lowest state in the initial parity sector, separately from the cost of remaining in that sector?
- **Exchange signal:** which reference process cancels an ordinary relative phase while retaining the statistical phase in the two-amplitude calculation?
