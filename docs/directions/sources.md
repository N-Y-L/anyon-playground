# Sources for these directions

These are starting points for the calculations and distinctions developed in the seven routes. Each chapter links its claims to the relevant paper; this list lets you choose a longer reading path. The [sources for the main anyon notes](../references.html) remain a separate guide.

## The single-particle saddle

- [Hegde, Subramanyan, Bradlyn and Vishveshwara, *Quasinormal modes and the Hawking–Unruh effect in quantum Hall systems* (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.08803). The inverted-oscillator connection to scattering and outgoing resonances.
- [Subramanyan, Hegde, Vishveshwara and Bradlyn, *Physics of the inverted harmonic oscillator* (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.09875). The extended treatment, including guiding centers, squeezing and scattering.
- [Stone, *An analogue of Hawking radiation in the quantum Hall effect* (2013)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.2317). A specified many-fermion state behind a thermal outgoing description.
- [Andrade e Silva and Jacobson, *Guiding center quantization of a quantum Hall analog of Hawking radiation* (2026, arXiv version 1)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00158v1). A re-examination of the guiding-center analogue and its interpretation.

## Detectors and statistical inference

- [Vishveshwara, *Revisiting the Hanbury Brown–Twiss set-up for fractional statistics* (2003)](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0304568). A two-source, two-sink edge-current proposal.
- [Nakamura, Liang, Gardner and Manfra, *Direct observation of anyonic braiding statistics* (2020)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-020-1019-1). Fractional quantum Hall interferometry at filling one third.
- [Rosenow, Levkivskyi and Halperin, *Current correlations from a mesoscopic anyon collider* (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.08470). A theory of dilute-beam current correlations.
- [Rosenow and Halperin, *Braids and beams: exploring fractional statistics with mesoscopic anyon colliders* (2025, version 2)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04319v2). The Poisson counting factor and time-domain braiding interpretation.
- [Feldman and Halperin, *Fractional charge and fractional statistics in the quantum Hall effects* (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.08998). Definitions, measurement logic and a review of experiments through its publication date.

## Wires and ramps

- [Kitaev, *Unpaired Majorana fermions in quantum wires* (2001)](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0010440). The chain and its boundary modes.
- [Hegde and Vishveshwara, *Majorana wave-function oscillations, fermion parity switches, and disorder in Kitaev chains* (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.03394). The finite-wire crossing pattern and its relation to localization.
- [DeGottardi, Sen and Vishveshwara, *Majorana fermions in superconducting 1D systems having periodic, quasiperiodic, and disordered potentials* (2013)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.0015). Transfer matrices and the normal-chain localization criterion.
- [Hegde, Shivamoggi, Vishveshwara and Sen, *Quench dynamics and parity blocking in Majorana wires* (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.5255). Dynamics constrained by conserved parity.
- [Jansen, Ruskai and Seiler, *Bounds for the adiabatic approximation with applications to quantum computation* (2007)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0603175). Conditions and quantitative bounds for adiabatic following.

## Closed surfaces

- [Haldane, *Fractional quantization of the Hall effect: a hierarchy of incompressible quantum fluid states* (1983)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.51.605). The sphere geometry for quantum Hall states.
- [Wen and Zee, *Shift and spin vector: new topological quantum numbers for the Hall fluids* (1992)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.69.953). The shift and geometric response.
- [Padavić and collaborators, *Vortex-antivortex physics in shell-shaped Bose–Einstein condensates*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.13030). A route from the winding count to condensate vortex energetics and dynamics.

## Nonorientable surfaces

- [Beugeling, Quelle and Morais Smith, *Nontrivial topological states on a Möbius band* (2014)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.6998). Compatible electronic models, local Hall regions and quantum spin Hall constructions.
- [Chan, Teo and Ryu, *Topological phases on non-orientable surfaces: twisting by parity symmetry* (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.03920). How the identification acts on anyon types.

## Other exchange problems

- [Kwan and collaborators, *Realization of 1D anyons with arbitrary statistical phase*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01737). Density-dependent hopping in a cold-atom lattice.
- [Wang and Levin, *Braiding statistics of loop excitations in three dimensions* (2014)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.7437). The three-loop braiding process.
- [Lin and Levin, *Loop braiding statistics in exactly soluble three-dimensional lattice models* (2015)](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.92.035115). Concrete models with distinct three-loop statistics.
- [Kitaev, *Anyons in an exactly solved model and beyond* (2006)](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0506438). An explicit lattice model and the mathematical structure of fusion and braiding.
