Sources for these directions

These are starting points for the calculations and distinctions developed in the seven routes. Each chapter links its claims to the relevant paper; this list lets you choose a longer reading path. The sources for the main anyon notes remain a separate guide.

The single-particle saddle

  • Hegde, Subramanyan, Bradlyn and Vishveshwara, Quasinormal modes and the Hawking–Unruh effect in quantum Hall systems (2019). The inverted-oscillator connection to scattering and outgoing resonances.
  • Subramanyan, Hegde, Vishveshwara and Bradlyn, Physics of the inverted harmonic oscillator (2021). The extended treatment, including guiding centers, squeezing and scattering.
  • Stone, An analogue of Hawking radiation in the quantum Hall effect (2013). A specified many-fermion state behind a thermal outgoing description.
  • Andrade e Silva and Jacobson, Guiding center quantization of a quantum Hall analog of Hawking radiation (2026, arXiv version 1). A re-examination of the guiding-center analogue and its interpretation.

Detectors and statistical inference

  • Vishveshwara, Revisiting the Hanbury Brown–Twiss set-up for fractional statistics (2003). A two-source, two-sink edge-current proposal.
  • Nakamura, Liang, Gardner and Manfra, Direct observation of anyonic braiding statistics (2020). Fractional quantum Hall interferometry at filling one third.
  • Rosenow, Levkivskyi and Halperin, Current correlations from a mesoscopic anyon collider (2016). A theory of dilute-beam current correlations.
  • Rosenow and Halperin, Braids and beams: exploring fractional statistics with mesoscopic anyon colliders (2025, version 2). The Poisson counting factor and time-domain braiding interpretation.
  • Feldman and Halperin, Fractional charge and fractional statistics in the quantum Hall effects (2021). Definitions, measurement logic and a review of experiments through its publication date.

Wires and ramps

  • Kitaev, Unpaired Majorana fermions in quantum wires (2001). The chain and its boundary modes.
  • Hegde and Vishveshwara, Majorana wave-function oscillations, fermion parity switches, and disorder in Kitaev chains (2016). The finite-wire crossing pattern and its relation to localization.
  • DeGottardi, Sen and Vishveshwara, Majorana fermions in superconducting 1D systems having periodic, quasiperiodic, and disordered potentials (2013). Transfer matrices and the normal-chain localization criterion.
  • Hegde, Shivamoggi, Vishveshwara and Sen, Quench dynamics and parity blocking in Majorana wires (2015). Dynamics constrained by conserved parity.
  • Jansen, Ruskai and Seiler, Bounds for the adiabatic approximation with applications to quantum computation (2007). Conditions and quantitative bounds for adiabatic following.

Closed surfaces

  • Haldane, Fractional quantization of the Hall effect: a hierarchy of incompressible quantum fluid states (1983). The sphere geometry for quantum Hall states.
  • Wen and Zee, Shift and spin vector: new topological quantum numbers for the Hall fluids (1992). The shift and geometric response.
  • Padavić and collaborators, Vortex-antivortex physics in shell-shaped Bose–Einstein condensates. A route from the winding count to condensate vortex energetics and dynamics.

Nonorientable surfaces

  • Beugeling, Quelle and Morais Smith, Nontrivial topological states on a Möbius band (2014). Compatible electronic models, local Hall regions and quantum spin Hall constructions.
  • Chan, Teo and Ryu, Topological phases on non-orientable surfaces: twisting by parity symmetry (2016). How the identification acts on anyon types.

Other exchange problems

  • Kwan and collaborators, Realization of 1D anyons with arbitrary statistical phase. Density-dependent hopping in a cold-atom lattice.
  • Wang and Levin, Braiding statistics of loop excitations in three dimensions (2014). The three-loop braiding process.
  • Lin and Levin, Loop braiding statistics in exactly soluble three-dimensional lattice models (2015). Concrete models with distinct three-loop statistics.
  • Kitaev, Anyons in an exactly solved model and beyond (2006). An explicit lattice model and the mathematical structure of fusion and braiding.
