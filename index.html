Anyons: visual physics notes

Neil Yuanting Li

An electron fluid can carry a movable defect with a fraction of an electron’s charge. Exchanging two such defects need not give the electrons’ minus sign. What is the object being moved—and how does its exchange rule become a measured signal?

From an electron fluid to a two-particle experiment →

The connected notes construct a quasihole in a many-electron wavefunction, calculate its charge and statistical phase, then follow two effective particles through preparation, a saddle potential and a detector comparison. Three embedded figures compare the same anyons in different states, the resulting quadratic dynamics, and two references for an extended collider.
