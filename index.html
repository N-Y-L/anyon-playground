Anyons and quantum transport

Neil Yuanting Li

These notes develop the physics of anyons from quasiparticles and exchange statistics to their preparation, motion and detection. The assumed background is graduate mathematics, basic graduate quantum mechanics and very basic condensed-matter physics. Definitions are introduced as needed; longer derivations can be expanded.

Start reading

An introduction to anyons explains why two dimensions permit different exchange rules, how collective excitations realize them, and how a change of quantum state becomes measurable. It develops microscopic examples, fractional and non-Abelian statistics, and the physical questions behind their study.

Preparation, motion and detection continues with localized pairs, motion through a saddle potential and extended colliders. These detailed calculations lead to comparisons of preparation and measurement.

Further reading

  • Selected papers: five studies of statistics, pair correlations and measurement, including two by Vishveshwara and collaborators.
  • Further directions: readout, protection and preparation, and the effect of geometry.
  • References for the derivations and calculations.

Independent calculations

  • Exchange paths and interference
  • Berry phase and localized-pair correlations
  • Toric-code strings and topological memory
  • Abelian theories, fusion paths, fusion bases and Ising braids

Each calculation specifies its model and assumptions. The companion quantum-transport notes cover currents and single-particle interference.
