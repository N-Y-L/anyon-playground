Anyons and quantum transport

Neil Yuanting Li

These notes develop the physics of anyons from quasiparticles and exchange statistics to their preparation, motion and detection. The assumed background is graduate mathematics, basic graduate quantum mechanics and very basic condensed-matter physics. Definitions are introduced as needed; longer derivations can be expanded.

Notes

Anyons and quantum transport is the main text. Its first sections cover:

  1. Quasiparticles
  2. Laughlin quasiholes and fractional charge
  3. Exchange statistics and Berry phase
  4. Non-Abelian exchanges
  5. Interference measurements

The later sections examine two-particle preparation, motion through a saddle potential and extended colliders. They lead to questions about preparation and measurement.

Further reading

  • Selected papers: microscopic statistics, interferometry, non-Abelian exchange and collider measurements.
  • Further directions: detectors, saddles, wire end modes, geometry and driven systems.
  • References for the derivations and calculations.

Independent calculations

  • Exchange paths and interference
  • Berry phase and localized-pair correlations
  • Toric-code strings and topological memory
  • Abelian theories, fusion paths, fusion bases and Ising braids

Each calculation specifies its model and assumptions. The companion quantum-transport notes cover currents and single-particle interference.
