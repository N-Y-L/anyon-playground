## Laughlin quasihole density

`fulsebakke-2023-quasihole-density.svg` reproduces panel **11(a)** from:

Jørgen Fulsebakke, Mikael Fremling, Niall Moran and J. K. Slingerland,
“Parametrization and thermodynamic scaling of pair correlation functions for the
fractional quantum Hall effect,” *SciPost Physics* **14**, 149 (2023).
[Paper and DOI](https://doi.org/10.21468/SciPostPhys.14.6.149) ·
[Author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.00371) ·
[Published PDF](https://inspirehep.net/files/1cd9bc7d3cf0bdd3b510c1f121ab7924)

The panel was cropped from page 18 of the published PDF and converted to SVG,
preserving the original vector curves, axes, legend and panel label. No data were
redrawn, interpolated or recolored. Accessible title/source metadata were added; the default display size was enlarged.
The graphic has no embedded raster images or external dependencies.

The source's `g(r)` is its dimensionless **electron-density profile** in this
panel, with a bulk reference at one. Its `ℓ` denotes the electron magnetic length
(`ℓ_B` in the notes). The finite systems are spherical and use chord distance;
the dashed curve is the thermodynamic extrapolation. This figure illustrates the
quasihole core and nearby density oscillations. It does not show compensation at
a finite droplet edge or independently evaluate the fractional-charge integral.
